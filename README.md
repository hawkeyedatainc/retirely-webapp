# Canada Citizenship Prep 2026 Website

Product, support, privacy, and terms site for Canada Citizenship Prep 2026 by HawkEye Data Solutions Inc.

## What is included

- App Store-oriented landing page
- Feature sections for timed mock tests, study lessons, AI Focus Review, flagged review, local civics, themes, and premium notes
- FAQ with independence and no-guarantee disclosures
- Support/contact page
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Use

## Important positioning

Canada Citizenship Prep 2026 is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, or operated by IRCC, the Government of Canada, or any government agency. Practice questions are not official exam questions, and the app does not guarantee passing.
